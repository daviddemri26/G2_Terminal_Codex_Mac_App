01Set up on your Mac+
  1. Check compatibility. The bridge is reviewed for Codex Mac 26.915.31945, build 9922 and Even Terminal 0.10.4. The linked guide has the current requirements.
  2. Install Even Terminal for Codex Mac App. Use the source installer linked in the full guide. This public alpha needs macOS 14 or later and Apple Command Line Tools with Swift 6 and Python 3; it builds locally.
  3. Connect your network. New guided setup uses Tailscale on Mac and phone. Existing Even Terminal LAN or interface profiles can be preserved; those variants have not been physically validated here.
  4. Pair in Terminal Mode. Open Even Terminal for Codex Mac App on the Mac, choose Connect → Show pairing code, and scan that QR in Terminal Mode in the Even Realities phone app. Keep the code private.
02Use your conversations+

Open Even Terminal separately and choose a conversation you recognize. Read replies and send a follow-up in the same conversation. Supported questions and approval choices appear there; complex controls remain on the Mac.

Keep the Mac awake and logged in, with Codex and your configured network available. Closing the Even Terminal for Codex Mac App window leaves its background service running.

In Even Terminal for Codex Mac App’s Text section, choose timestamps, progress updates, and paragraph spacing. The original display is the default.

03If something stops working+

Start with Overview on the Mac. Check the Codex version, bridge status, and the configured network on both devices. A local “Ready” status does not confirm the phone-to-glasses connection.

After an uncertain send, check the conversation in Codex before retrying. Sending again may create a second instruction.

If a LAN/interface address changes, finish the interaction, restart the bridge when safe, then show and scan a new QR. Refreshing the QR alone is insufficient.

04About this guide+

Made for Even G2 owners who work in the Codex Mac app and want to keep using those conversations from Even Terminal.

This guide stores its instructions in the app. It needs no Mac connection or additional account, requests no device permissions, and does not collect analytics, read conversations, or send prompts. External links need an internet connection and have their own privacy policies.

On the glasses, scroll through four topics and tap to read. Double-tap a topic to go back; double-tap the main menu for the system exit confirmation. Instructions are also available on this phone screen.

Even Terminal for Codex Mac App and this companion are independent community projects, not official OpenAI or Even Realities products. Installing this guide does not install the Mac bridge or Even Terminal.
